# Decords GSC Growth Batch 17

Date: 2026-08-30  
Store: https://www.decords.com/  
Search Console source period: 2026-07-31 to 2026-08-27

## Objective

Batch 17 targeted four product landing pages that:

- had measurable Google Search impressions;
- ranked mainly on the first or second results page;
- had weak click-through rates relative to their positions;
- had not received a substantive rewrite in batches 1–16;
- still contained ambiguous sizing, incomplete installation guidance, or unsupported product claims.

The work was restricted to product titles, HTML descriptions, tags, SEO titles, and meta descriptions. Product IDs, handles, URLs, statuses, product types, vendors, variants, options, SKUs, prices, inventory, media, image order, alt text, translations, and collection membership were preserved.
