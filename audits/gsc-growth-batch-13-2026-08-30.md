# Decords GSC Growth Batch 13

Date: 2026-08-30
Store: https://www.decords.com/
Search Console source period: 2026-07-31 to 2026-08-27

## Selection method

Batch 13 continued the query-led product-page optimization after Batch 12. Candidate landing pages were selected from settled Google Search Console query and page data, checked against the existing GitHub audit archive, and verified against live Shopify records before editing.

Recently rewritten pages were excluded so that fresh changes would not be overwritten before Google had recrawled and evaluated them. The Gothic masquerade mask and cyberpunk half mask were removed from the final batch because their live Shopify records had already been substantially updated on 2026-08-28 or 2026-08-29. Mailbox decal and picture-frame sticker candidates were also excluded because their current descriptions were already complete and internally linked.

The final batch contained four older pages with meaningful impressions, weak CTR, and clear content or intent problems:

1. A generic cat-face decal whose legacy URL and earlier SEO language incorrectly implied a Pallas cat or manul design.
2. A large world-map decal with country names and high impressions but extremely low CTR.
3. A Halloween cat window decal whose styled imagery could make buyers think the vinyl glows or contains lighting.
4. A gold floral line-art decal whose earlier copy used unsupported removable, repositionable, and residue-free claims.

## Completed Shopify changes

### 1. Cat Face Wall Decal

Old product title:

`Cat Face Wall Decal | Large Cat Eyes Wall Sticker`

New product title:

`Cat Face Wall Decal | Large Cat Eyes Vinyl Sticker`

Explicit SEO title:

`Large Cat Face Wall Decal | Cat Eyes Vinyl Sticker`

SEO title length: 50 characters.

Meta description:

`Large cat face wall decal printed in black on transparent adhesive vinyl. Choose 8–118 inches by longest side; oversized designs arrive in aligned sections.`

Meta description length: 156 characters.

Changes:

- Reframed the page around generic cat-face, cat-eyes, feline-wall-art, and cat-wall-decal intent.
- Removed the false implication that the artwork is a species-specific Pallas cat or manul illustration.
- Clarified that the artwork is printed in black on transparent adhesive vinyl.
- Explained contrast limitations on dark, patterned, textured, or strongly colored surfaces.
- Explained that the selected option is the longest side and that the other dimension scales proportionally.
- Added sectioning information for designs exceeding 24 inches on either side.
- Added realistic installation, surface-testing, care, removal, and included-item information.
- Added links to Animals & Birds, Wall Stickers, and the related Halloween Cat Window Decal.
- Replaced the old tag set with 15 exact cat-decal and material-intent tags.

The legacy handle `pallas-cat-wall-decal-manul-cat-with-64422` was deliberately retained. Changing it would have created an avoidable URL migration and redirect while the page already has impressions and index history. The title, SEO title, description, and tags now define the actual product intent.

## Live on-page audit observation

The GSC Wizard live crawler requested the four `.com` product URLs after publication. Shopify Markets returned a 302 redirect to the equivalent `.de` URLs for that crawler's geographic context. The final responses were HTTP 200 and contained:

- no `noindex` directive;
- one H1 per page;
- hreflang markup;
- Product, Offer, BreadcrumbList, shipping, return-policy, and related structured-data types;
- no structured-data validation issues reported by the audit.

Because the crawler was routed to the German market, the live audit classified the requested `.com` URLs as canonicalized to `.de` and did not directly validate the default English `.com` rendering. Google URL Inspection nevertheless reports the four `.com` URLs as submitted, indexed, and indexing allowed. Domain and market routing were not changed in this content batch.

The live audit also counted 12 missing alt attributes among 46 total page images on each rendered `.de` page. These counts include theme and shared storefront imagery, not only the product gallery. The Shopify product-media audit showed the existing product gallery alt fields were preserved. This broader theme-level alt issue should be handled separately rather than by changing product images in this batch.
