## Selection method

This batch used Search Console query and landing-page data to identify pages with established impressions, positions generally within or close to page one, and weak click-through rate.

Pages modified from 2026-08-27 through 2026-08-30 were excluded because the available Search Console period ends on 2026-08-27. Rewriting those pages again would remove the ability to evaluate the previous changes after Google recrawls them.

Examples excluded for this reason included recently updated mushroom shelves, office wall art, window collections, kitchen, garage, vintage, nature, space, costume, cute, cat, gym, world map, rock shelf, Kitsune mask, car decal, floor decal and other product clusters.

## Daisy duplication decision

The compact and extended white Daisy pages use the same underlying gallery and design, but they were not consolidated because their commercial configurations differ materially:

- The compact page has six size choices and the lower price structure.
- The extended page has sixteen size choices, including intermediate and large diameters up to 24 inches.
- Exact Shopify order searches by representative SKU returned no orders for either page.

Archiving one page would select one price and option strategy. The safe SEO action was therefore to separate search intent and cross-link the offers rather than delete or redirect either product.

The custom-colour Daisy page remains separate because it allows a selectable main flower colour and uses a yellow centre by default.
