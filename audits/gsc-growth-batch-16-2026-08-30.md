## Live crawler observation

The on-page crawler followed a Shopify Markets 302 redirect from each `.com` URL to the corresponding `.de` URL. It therefore audited German-market canonical and localized metadata rather than the requested English `.com` pages.

Consequences:

- the crawler marked the requested `.com` pages as canonicalized;
- German-market title and meta-length warnings appeared for three pages;
- these warnings do not represent a failed write to the English Shopify product records;
- Google URL Inspection independently confirmed that all four exact `.com` URLs are submitted, indexed, and indexing-allowed.

No Shopify Markets, domain, canonical, hreflang, or German-language settings were changed in this batch.
