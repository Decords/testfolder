### Floor stickers collection

Collection title changed to:

`Floor Stickers & 3D Vinyl Floor Decals`

### Frosted wave glass decal

Product title changed to:

`Glass Door Decal | Frosted Wave Privacy Film`

Full product description was rewritten to clarify:

- Only the frosted wave shapes are supplied.
- Clear glass remains between and around the waves.
- The design provides partial visual separation, not complete privacy.
- Available sizes range from 24 x 24 to 150 x 150 inches.
- Available finishes are Regular Etch Glass, Heavy Etch and Blue Sparkle.
- Glass compatibility, measurement, installation, wet-area limits, care and removal must be checked.

The product was removed from the incorrect `3D Open Window Wall Decals` collection. Five image alt texts were rewritten without changing image order or files.
