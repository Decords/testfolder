# Decords GSC Growth Batch 19

Date: 2026-08-31  
Store: https://www.decords.com/  
Search Console source period: 2026-07-31 to 2026-08-27

## Objective

Batch 19 continued the English-language Google Search Console growth workflow after cross-checking the candidate list against batches 1–18.

The final selection contained four previously unprocessed commercial product pages with established Google visibility, weak click-through rates, outdated descriptions, unclear ordering logic or unsupported product claims.

The Shopify write scope was limited to:

- product title;
- HTML product description;
- product tags;
- SEO title;
- meta description.

The operation did not write product handles, customer-facing URLs, product status, product type, vendor, variants, option values, SKUs, barcodes, prices, compare-at prices, inventory, media, image order, alt text, collections or translations.

## Selection and exclusions

The following four pages were selected:

1. Urban street 3D-effect door mural.
2. Twenty-piece boho vinyl stripe set.
3. Custom etched-look mirror decal.
4. Pack of 168 PE protective-earth identification labels.

The personalized carbon-fiber name-decal product was retained as a candidate for Batch 20. The pastel boho headboard decal was excluded because it had already been updated on 2026-08-28 and should not be overwritten before post-change measurement.
