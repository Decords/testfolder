## Selection method

This batch continued the query-led SEO work after Batch 11. Candidate landing pages were selected from settled Google Search Console query and page data, then checked against the existing audit archive and live Shopify records.

Pages substantially edited on 2026-08-29 or 2026-08-30 were excluded from another full rewrite. This prevented the current batch from overwriting recent work before Google had recrawled and evaluated it. High-impression candidates excluded for this reason included the floating mushroom shelf, clear mailing labels, office wall art, front-door welcome decal, floor stickers, stair-riser stickers, vehicle pillar decals, hood decals, botanical decals, daisy decals and several recently optimized 3D products.

Four product pages remained suitable. None of their handles appeared in the previous audit reports:

1. A physical ribbed pencil holder receiving impressions for `3d print pencil holder`.
2. A dripping wall shelf receiving impressions for `drip shelf`.
3. A full Kitsune mask receiving impressions for `kitsune fox spirit costume`.
4. A Kitsune half mask receiving the same query and competing with the full-mask page.

The mask pair was treated as an intent-separation problem rather than two independent keyword rewrites. The full-mask page was assigned full-face terminology, while the half-mask page was assigned explicit half-mask and open lower-face terminology.

- photographed stationery and brushes are not included;

The title, SEO title, first paragraph and tags now use the actual query concepts `3D printed pencil holder`, `ribbed pencil holder`, `desk pen cup`, `makeup brush cup` and `desk organizer` without suggesting that a digital model file is being sold.

- it is supplied unpainted;

## Monitoring plan

The four pages should be evaluated only after Google recrawls them and Search Console contains settled post-change data.

Primary query-page pairs to monitor:

1. `3d print pencil holder` → ribbed pencil-holder product.
2. `drip shelf` → melting wall-shelf product.
3. `kitsune fox spirit costume` → full Kitsune mask.
4. `kitsune fox spirit costume` → Kitsune half mask.

For the mask pair, the evaluation should not look only at combined impressions. It should check whether broader full-face terms increasingly select the full-mask page and half-mask terms increasingly select the half-mask page. A reduction in duplicate impressions can be positive if each page gains clearer relevance for its own format.

Recently optimized pages from this and earlier batches should not be rewritten again during the observation period unless a factual error, broken page or commercial problem is found.
