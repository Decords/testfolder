Date: 2026-08-30
Store: https://www.decords.com/

## Selection method

Batch 12 continued the query-led SEO work after Batch 11. Candidate pages were selected from settled Google Search Console query and landing-page data, checked against the existing audit archive, and then verified against current Shopify records.

Pages substantially edited on 2026-08-29 or 2026-08-30 were excluded from another rewrite. This prevented new work from overwriting recent changes before Google had recrawled and evaluated them. Excluded candidates included the floating mushroom shelf, clear mailing labels, office wall art, front-door welcome decal, floor stickers, stair-riser stickers, vehicle pillar decals, hood decals, botanical decals and several recently optimized 3D products.

Four products remained suitable and none of their handles appeared in the previous audit reports:

1. A physical ribbed pencil holder receiving impressions for `3d print pencil holder`.
2. A dripping wall shelf receiving impressions for `drip shelf`.
3. A full Kitsune mask receiving impressions for `kitsune fox spirit costume`.
4. A Kitsune half mask receiving the same query and competing with the full-mask page.

The two Kitsune pages were treated as an intent-separation problem. The full mask now uses full-face and full-coverage terminology. The half mask now uses half-mask and open lower-face terminology.

### Contradictions removed

The previous description said both that the mask was delivered only in white and that it was produced in the color selected by the buyer. Shopify currently contains 16 selectable solid-color variants, so the white-only statement was incorrect.

The previous description also described measurements as required and later as optional. It contained both a 3–5 business-day production statement and a 1–3 business-day statement.

The rewritten page now consistently states that:

- this is the half-mask version;
- the lower-face area remains more open than on the full mask;
- the buyer chooses an available solid filament color;
- the mask is not limited to white;
- it is supplied unpainted;
- painted details visible in presentation images are not automatically included;
- paint and painting tools are not included;
- measurements are optional;
- the standard adult size is produced when measurements are omitted;
- the order includes one half mask and elastic straps;
- wigs, clothing and other photographed accessories are excluded;
- it is a fixed design rather than a face scan or custom mould;
- it is not protective equipment;
- production normally takes 1–3 business days before dispatch.

The page now links directly to the full-mask product and to the 3D printed collection.

### Smart collection correction

The half-mask previously did not belong to `3D Printed Home Decor, Wall Art & Gifts`, because its former title did not contain `3D Printed`. The smart collection uses a title-based rule requiring that phrase.

After the corrected title was saved and Shopify completed its automated collection evaluation, the half-mask was confirmed as a member of:

- `Decords Online Store / All Products`;
- `3D Printed Home Decor, Wall Art & Gifts`;
- the internal performance-tracking collection.

No manual collection override was required.

## Monitoring plan

Evaluate these pages after Google recrawls them and Search Console contains settled post-change data.

Primary query-page pairs:

1. `3d print pencil holder` → ribbed pencil-holder product.
2. `drip shelf` → melting wall-shelf product.
3. `kitsune fox spirit costume` → full Kitsune mask.
4. `kitsune fox spirit costume` → Kitsune half mask.

For the mask pair, evaluation should inspect page selection rather than only combined impressions. Broader full-face terms should increasingly select the full-mask page, while half-mask terms should increasingly select the half-mask page. A reduction in duplicate impressions can be positive if each page becomes clearer for its own product format.

These four pages should not be rewritten again during the observation period unless a factual error, broken page or commercial problem is found.
