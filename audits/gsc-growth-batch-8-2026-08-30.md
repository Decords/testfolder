## 1. Vinyl letter and number sticker cluster

### Audit conclusion

The three active listings did not represent three equal offers.

- Product `7348340982010` uses a six-option large-character range and remains a separate large-letter product.
- Product `8747104665938` is the broader 168-piece character assortment and remains the main compact/general set.
- Product `8755074826578` duplicated the 168-piece product with the same 16-option structure and price grid. It was archived rather than deleted.

- Exterior, wet and regularly washed installations require material and surface confirmation.
- Surface preparation, baseline layout, spacing, care and removal limitations.

## 3. Photo collage wallpaper mural

Product ID: `gid://shopify/Product/8747444797778`
Handle preserved: `photo-collage-picture-wallpaper-sticker-95222`

New title:

`Photo Collage Wallpaper Mural | Picture Wall Design`

New product type:

`Photo Collage Wall Murals`

### Product-definition correction

The previous page could be interpreted as an automatic service that prints a new collage from customer photographs. The current option structure does not support that interpretation by itself.

The rewritten page now states that:

- The standard order prints the gallery collage design.
- Customer photographs are not automatically converted into a custom collage after checkout.
- A personal-photo project requires file review, dimensions, a separate quotation and proof approval.
- The `Sample: 12 x 8 inches` option is a print and material reference, not a full wall mural.
- The `Custom size: 12 x 12 inches` option is a pricing unit used only after Decords calculates the required quantity.
- Standard sizes describe the approximate full mural width and height.
- Large murals can be supplied in numbered panels.
- Crop, aspect ratio, image quality, seams and installation sequence must be planned before production.

Collection added:

- Vinyl Wallpapers / Adhesive Wallpaper Decals.

Search signal used:

- `custom collage wallpaper`: 44 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 13.39.

The page can still support a separately confirmed custom-photo project, but it no longer promises one by default.
