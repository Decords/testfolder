## Scope and selection

## Shopify changes

### 1. Glow Drawing Board for Kids

Old product title:

`Light Drawing Board For Kids - The Glow In Dark Neon Effect - Fun Magic Developing Toy With Led Sketch Lightboard Gift`

New product title:

`Glow Drawing Board for Kids | Reusable Light Drawing Pad`

SEO title:

`Glow Drawing Board for Kids | Light Drawing Pad`

Meta description:

`Reusable glow drawing board with an included light pen. Choose A4 or A3, with or without a gift box. Drawings brighten, then gradually fade for repeated use.`

Main corrections and improvements:

- Reframed the page around glow drawing board, light drawing pad, neon drawing pad, and light-pen drawing intent.
- Clarified that the product is a physical plastic board covered with glow-in-the-dark vinyl.
- Clarified that the board itself contains no internal electronics and is not an LCD tablet, digital download, permanent drawing surface, or night light.
- Removed conflicting universal claims that drawings fade after 10, 20, or 30–40 minutes.
- Explained that brightness and fade time depend on exposure time, pen distance, angle, ambient light, and room conditions.
- Removed unsupported health, environmental, and child-development claims.
- Preserved the instruction not to look directly into the light pen or point it toward eyes.
- Added A4 and A3 dimensions and explained the with-box and without-box options.
- Added exact package contents, care, storage, and supervision guidance.
- Added two verified collection links.
- Installed 15 exact search-intent tags.

## Live on-page audit

A live crawler requested all four `.com` URLs. Shopify Markets geographically redirected the crawler with HTTP 302 responses to equivalent `.de` URLs carrying `shpxid` parameters. The audit consequently classified the requested `.com` URLs as canonicalized to `.de`.

This does not contradict Google URL Inspection, which independently confirmed all four exact `.com` URLs as submitted, indexed, allowed, and PASS. The routed pages had HTTP 200 final responses, no `noindex`, one H1 each, ten hreflang entries, viewport and favicon, Product/Offer/BreadcrumbList/shipping/return-policy schema, and zero structured-data issues.

The crawler saw the correct new SEO titles. It reported a 15-character meta description for the routed German light-board page, while Shopify GraphQL confirmed the new English meta description at 157 characters. This is a German-market localization observation, not a failure of the English update; no German translation was modified in this batch.

The rendered-page audit also reported 11–12 images without alt text out of 42–50 images. Those counts include theme and shared-site assets. Existing product media and alt text were intentionally preserved.
