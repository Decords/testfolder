# Decords GSC Growth Batch 14

Date: 2026-08-30
Store: https://www.decords.com/
Search Console source period: 2026-07-31 to 2026-08-27

## Selection method

Batch 14 continued query-led product-page optimization after Batch 13. Candidate landing pages were selected from Google Search Console page and query data, checked against the GitHub audit archive, and verified against live Shopify product records before editing.

High-impression candidates that had already been substantially updated between 2026-08-27 and 2026-08-30 were excluded. This prevented fresh changes from being overwritten before Google had time to crawl and evaluate them. Excluded examples included the 3D printed mushroom shelf, honeycomb hood decal, floor stickers page, custom mirror decal, Gothic mask pages, gaming controller decal, and several recently revised window-film and wallpaper products.

The final batch contained four older pages with relevant search visibility and clear content, CTR, or intent problems:

1. A glow drawing board page with a very long title, conflicting claims about fade time, unsupported health and environmental claims, and unclear differentiation from an electronic tablet.
2. A crescent moon wall cat bed page ranking for relevant cat-bed queries but needing stronger fit, installation, material, and wall-load guidance.
3. A Funny Warning sticker set with repetitive copy and weak differentiation from a near-identical Danger version.
4. A Funny Danger sticker set competing with the Warning page and lacking a distinct danger-sticker search focus.

### 1. Glow Drawing Board for Kids

Old product title:

`Light Drawing Board For Kids - The Glow In Dark Neon Effect - Fun Magic Developing Toy With Led Sketch Lightboard Gift`

New product title:

`Glow Drawing Board for Kids | Reusable Light Drawing Pad`

Product title length: 56 characters.

SEO title:

`Glow Drawing Board for Kids | Light Drawing Pad`

SEO title length: 47 characters.

Meta description:

`Reusable glow drawing board with an included light pen. Choose A4 or A3, with or without a gift box. Drawings brighten, then gradually fade for repeated use.`

Meta description length: 157 characters.

Changes:

- Reframed the product around glow drawing board, light drawing pad, neon drawing pad, and light-pen drawing intent.
- Clarified that the product is a physical plastic board covered with glow-in-the-dark vinyl.
- Clarified that the board itself contains no internal electronics and is not an LCD tablet, digital device, download, or night light.
- Removed conflicting universal claims that drawings fade after 10, 20, or 30–40 minutes.
- Replaced those claims with an accurate explanation that brightness and fade time depend on exposure time, pen distance, angle, ambient light, and room conditions.
- Removed unsupported claims such as certified harmless to eyes, non-toxic, environmentally friendly, guaranteed creative-development outcomes, defeating fear of darkness, and guaranteed replacement of gadgets.
- Preserved the safety instruction not to look directly into the light pen or point it toward eyes.
- Added exact A4 and A3 dimensions and explained the with-box and without-box variants.
- Added a clear included-items section: one board, one light pen, and supplied use guidance.
- Added care, storage, supervision, and physical-product clarification.
- Added links to Glow in the Dark Decals and Science & Education.
- Replaced the old tag set with 15 exact drawing-board, light-pad, glow-art, and size-intent tags.

## Live on-page audit

A live technical audit fetched all four `.com` URLs. Shopify Markets geographically redirected the crawler with HTTP 302 responses to equivalent `.de` URLs and added `shpxid` parameters. The crawler consequently reported the requested `.com` URLs as canonicalized to `.de`.

This does not contradict Google URL Inspection, which independently confirmed that all four exact `.com` URLs are submitted, indexed, allowed, and PASS. The routed pages also had:

- HTTP 200 final responses;
- no `noindex` directive;
- one H1 each;
- ten hreflang entries;
- viewport and favicon;
- Product, Offer, BreadcrumbList, shipping, return-policy, and related structured data;
- zero structured-data issues.

The live audit saw the correct new SEO titles on the routed pages. It reported a 15-character meta description for the routed German light-board page, while Shopify GraphQL confirmed the new English product meta description at 157 characters. This is a German-market localization observation, not a failure of the English Shopify update, and no German translation was changed in this batch.

The crawler also reported 11–12 images without alt text out of 42–50 images on the fully rendered pages. Those counts include theme and shared-site assets. Existing product media and their alt text were intentionally left unchanged in this content batch.
